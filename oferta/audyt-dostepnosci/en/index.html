Why shops ask for it

United Kingdom

The Equality Act 2010 asks service providers to make reasonable adjustments for disabled customers. WCAG 2.2 AA is the usual benchmark for websites.

United States

Website accessibility is regularly raised under the ADA. WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 AA is the reference most often used.

Selling to the EU

Since 28 June 2025 the European Accessibility Act covers e-commerce services for EU consumers. Microenterprises are exempt.

Whether a law applies to your shop is a question for a lawyer. The audit shows what to fix, and it makes buying easier for every customer.

FAQ

Do I get a certificate?

There is no official accessibility certificate for online shops. You get a dated report with the scope and results, which documents the state of the shop and the fixes made.

Do you need admin access?

Not for the audit. The shop address is enough: I test public pages and the checkout as a guest, without logging in or placing an order. Account areas behind a login only by separate agreement, through a test account you create for me. Admin access is only needed if you want me to implement the fixes, again through a separate user account; never share your own password.

Do you test with screen readers?

The audit covers keyboard use, zoom, small screens, contrast, forms and the code structure screen readers rely on (labels, headings, names of buttons and links). Testing with assistive technology users is not included.
