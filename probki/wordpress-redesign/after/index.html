Do I need planning permission for a rear extension?

Many single-storey rear extensions do not, but it depends on size and location. We check this at the site visit and tell you in writing.

Can you only do the calculations, without the build?

Yes. We prepare structural calculations and drawings for your own builder or architect for a fixed fee.

How long does a typical extension take?

We give you a schedule together with the price, after we have seen the site and agreed the drawings.
