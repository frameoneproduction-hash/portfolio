Why licensed matters in Texas

Texas law requires electrical work to be performed by TDLR-licensed electricians. Statewide, electricians averaged $35.07/hr in 2024 (Bureau of Labor Statistics) – skilled work with real accountability: license numbers you can verify, insurance, and permits pulled when the job needs one.
