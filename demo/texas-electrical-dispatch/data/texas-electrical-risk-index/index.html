Texas Electrical Risk Index

390 Texas cities ranked by residential electrical risk. The index combines four signals: share of homes built before 1980 (U.S. Census ACS), severe weather events over the last 10 years (NOAA Storm Events Database), electric heating dependency, and median housing age. Higher score = more electrical stress on the local housing stock.

Methodology

40%

Housing age

Share of housing units built before 1980 – pre-GFCI/AFCI code era. Source: Census ACS 5-year, "Year Structure Built".

30%

Storm exposure

Severe weather events (hail, thunderstorm wind, lightning, tornado, flood, ice) in the county, last 10 years. Source: NOAA Storm Events.

15%

Electric heat share

Homes heating with electricity stack winter load onto panels. Source: Census ACS "House Heating Fuel".

15%

Median year built

Older medians shift the whole stock toward earlier wiring standards. Source: Census ACS.

Each component is percentile-normalized across all 390 cities, weighted, and scaled 0–100. The index measures environmental and housing-stock stress factors; it is not a prediction of any individual home's condition. Population data: Census ACS. Open to republish with attribution.
