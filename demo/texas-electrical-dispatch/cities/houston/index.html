Hiring an Electrician in Houston? Check This First

This line routes calls to independent contractors. Whoever comes to your Houston address, check these four things before work begins:

  • Ask whether a permit is needed

    Panel changes and new circuits typically require one; the contractor handles the paperwork.

  • Get the estimate in writing

    Before work starts, not after. Any diagnostic or trip fee should be stated up front.

  • Confirm liability insurance

    Texas requires electrical contractors to carry coverage. Ask for the certificate.

  • Ask for the TDLR license number

    Every Texas electrician must carry one. Look it up in 30 seconds at tdlr.texas.gov.
