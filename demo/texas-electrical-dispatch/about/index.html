Who We Are (In Plain Words)

What this service is

Texas Electrical Dispatch Network is an advertising and call referral service. We publish local electrical-risk data for 390 Texas cities and list one phone number. When you call, the call is routed to an independent electrical service provider covering your area. We do not perform electrical work, employ electricians, or send our own trucks.

How we make money

This site is paid through an advertising partner for qualifying calls. That's the whole model. You never pay us anything, and the price you negotiate with the contractor is between you and them. We recommend getting every estimate in writing before authorizing work.

Where the data comes from

Our city pages and the Texas Electrical Risk Index are built from public sources: U.S. Census American Community Survey (housing age, heating fuel), NOAA Storm Events Database (10-year severe weather records), FEMA National Risk Index, and Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data. We cite sources on the page and publish the index as open data.

Important information

TX Electrical Dispatch is a free service to assist homeowners in connecting with local service providers. All contractors/providers are independent and TX Electrical Dispatch does not warrant or guarantee any work performed. It is the responsibility of the homeowner to verify that the hired contractor furnishes the necessary license and insurance required for the work being performed. All persons depicted in a photo or video are actors or models and not contractors listed on TX Electrical Dispatch.

Same-day and 24/7 emergency services are subject to provider participation, location, technician availability, and demand. Availability is not guaranteed and may vary by market and appointment capacity.

This site is an advertising and call referral service: when you call, the call is routed to an independent electrical service provider in your area, and this site is paid for qualifying calls.
