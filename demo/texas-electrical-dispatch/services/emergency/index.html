When Homeowners Need It

When You Need an Emergency Electrician in Texas

Certain electrical situations demand immediate professional attention. Recognizing these warning signs protects your family and prevents costly property damage.

Lights flashing throughout your home – not just a single fixture – often signals loose wiring, an overloaded circuit, or utility feed problems. Before assuming it's a grid issue, check the outage map power resources from your provider. If your neighbors have power but your electricity is out, the problem lies within your home's electrical system.

Burning smells or visible sparks from outlets, switches, or your breaker panel require immediate action. Disconnect power at the main breaker and call a licensed electrician. These symptoms indicate arcing, which causes thousands of house fires annually in Texas.

Frequent breaker trips point to overloaded circuits or failing breakers. While occasional trips happen, repeated issues – especially in older Texas homes – warrant emergency evaluation. Damaged breakers may fail to trip during actual overloads, creating fire hazards.

Complete power loss when utility service is active demands investigation. If you've checked whether CenterPoint power is out, verified Oncor power status, or confirmed AEP power conditions through their outage resources, yet your home remains dark, your main panel or service entrance may have failed.

Outlets or switches that are hot to the touch indicate dangerous wiring conditions that need prompt attention.

Water exposure to electrical systems – from flooding, burst pipes, or roof leaks – creates shock and fire risks. Never attempt to restore power yourself after water intrusion.

Questions Texans Ask

When should I call an emergency electrician in Texas?
Call immediately if you experience sparking outlets, burning electrical smells, exposed wiring, a main panel that won't reset, or complete loss of power when your utility's outage map shows no reported issues in your area. These situations pose fire or shock hazards requiring urgent attention from a licensed electrician.
How do I know if my power outage is a utility problem or a house problem?
Check your provider's energy outage map first. If CenterPoint, Oncor, or AEP shows power out in your neighborhood, wait for restoration. If neighbors have power but your lights are out, the issue is likely your electrical panel, main breaker, or service entrance – call the Texas Electrical Dispatch Network for diagnosis.
What does emergency electrical service cost in Texas?
Emergency calls typically range from $150 to $350 for the service visit, plus parts and labor for repairs. After-hours, weekend, and holiday calls may include additional fees. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.
How quickly can an emergency electrician arrive? Response varies by location and current demand. Q: What causes lights flashing throughout my house?
Flickering or flashing lights often indicate loose connections, a failing main breaker, or problems with your utility's transformer. Persistent flashing is a fire hazard – shut off affected circuits and call for emergency service.
How much does a Generac standby generator cost installed?
Whole-home standby generator installation (for example, a Generac unit) often runs roughly $8,000 to $15,000 or more in Texas, depending on generator size, fuel line, transfer switch, and electrical load requirements. This includes the unit, transfer switch, permits, and professional installation.
Do emergency electrical repairs require permits?
Most panel replacements, service upgrades, and significant rewiring require permits in Texas municipalities. Licensed electricians in our network handle permit applications and ensure all work meets the National Electrical Code and local requirements.
What is the cost to install a 240v outlet?
A 240v outlet installation typically costs $250 to $500, depending on distance from your panel and whether your electrical system needs circuit additions. This outlet type serves heavy appliances like dryers and ranges.
How much does ceiling fan installation cost?
The cost to install a new ceiling fan ranges from $150 to $350 when existing wiring is present. Installations requiring new wiring or structural support cost more. Never attempt ceiling fan wiring yourself – improper installation causes fires.
Should I reset a tripped breaker that keeps tripping?
Reset once. If it trips again immediately, stop – repeated tripping indicates a short circuit, ground fault, or overloaded circuit requiring professional diagnosis before safe restoration.
