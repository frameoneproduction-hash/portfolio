Your electrician will perform a load calculation to determine exact requirements. **Q: Do I need a permit for home standby generator installation?** A: Yes. Texas requires electrical permits for backup power generator installations. Your licensed electrician handles permit applications, inspections, and code compliance documentation through your local municipality. **Q: How long does generator installation take?** A: Professional installation typically requires one to two days. This includes setting the concrete pad, connecting the automatic transfer switch, running gas lines, and completing electrical integration with your main panel. **Q: Will my generator start automatically during a power outage?** A: Yes. A Generac automatic standby generator monitors utility power continuously.
