Before Any San Antonio Electrician Starts Work

This site routes calls to independent electrical service providers; it does not do the work. Hold whoever shows up, whether you found them here or anywhere else, to the same standard:

  • Get the estimate in writing

    Price, scope and fees on paper before the first breaker comes off.

  • Ask whether a permit is needed

    Permitted work gets inspected. Skipping the permit can cause trouble at resale or with insurance.

  • Confirm liability insurance

    If something goes wrong on the job, their insurance pays, not yours. Ask for the certificate.

  • Ask for the TDLR license number

    Every Texas electrician must carry one. Look it up in 30 seconds at tdlr.texas.gov.
