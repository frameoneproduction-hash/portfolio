    Consent Mode v2

    • 01
      Default first

      A consent default with everything denied is pushed before GTM loads any tag. Then the banner choice sends a consent update.

    • 02
      Four signals

      analytics_storage and ad_storage for cookies, ad_user_data (sending user data to Google for ads) and ad_personalization (personalised ads and remarketing) – the two Google added in v2.

    • 03
      Basic or advanced

      Basic: Google tags wait for consent. Advanced: tags load with consent denied and send cookieless pings. We pick one with you, based on your privacy policy and ads needs.

    • 04
      Works with your banner

      Your cookie banner (CMP) sends the update; I check in Tag Assistant that no Google tag fires before the default.

    What I check in a tracking audit

    • Purchase counted twice after a thank-you page reload or a payment redirect
    • value sent without currency, so revenue is missing in reports
    • Consent default missing, or set after the first Google tag
    • Payment gateways stealing the session source (unwanted referrals)
    • Your own team’s visits counted as customers (internal traffic)
    • Personal data such as email addresses in page URLs sent to GA4
    • Old Universal Analytics or duplicate GA4 tags still on the site
