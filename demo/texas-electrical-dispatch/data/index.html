Important information

TX Electrical Dispatch is a free service to assist homeowners in connecting with local service providers. All contractors/providers are independent and TX Electrical Dispatch does not warrant or guarantee any work performed. It is the responsibility of the homeowner to verify that the hired contractor furnishes the necessary license and insurance required for the work being performed. All persons depicted in a photo or video are actors or models and not contractors listed on TX Electrical Dispatch.

Same-day and 24/7 emergency services are subject to provider participation, location, technician availability, and demand. Availability is not guaranteed and may vary by market and appointment capacity.

We are an advertising and lead coordination service: when you call, you are connected with an independent Texas-licensed electrical contractor in our network, and we earn a fee when calls connect. We do not perform electrical work. Before authorizing work, verify the provider's license at tdlr.texas.gov, confirm insurance coverage, and get the cost estimate in writing.
