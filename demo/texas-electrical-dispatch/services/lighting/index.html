When should lighting work be done by an electrician?

New fixtures where no box exists, recessed lights, outdoor and landscape lighting on line voltage, and any flicker or dimming that points to a wiring fault. In Texas, paid electrical work, including new circuits, must be done by a TDLR-licensed electrician.

Questions Texans Ask

Do I need a licensed electrician to install new lighting fixtures in my Texas home?
Yes. Texas law requires a licensed electrician for electrical work beyond simple fixture swaps. Professional installation ensures code compliance, proper load calculations, and warranty protection. The Texas Electrical Dispatch Network connects you with verified, state-licensed professionals.
How much does ceiling fan installation cost in Texas?
The cost to install a new ceiling fan typically ranges from $150 to $350 for standard installations with existing wiring. New circuit runs or high ceilings increase costs. Your estimate includes labor, mounting hardware, and post-installation testing.
What is the process for installing bath lighting fixtures?
Installation begins with a site assessment to evaluate existing wiring and ventilation. Your electrician disconnects power, removes old fixtures, and installs new bath vanity lights or a bath vent fan with light. The process typically takes two to four hours depending on complexity.
Do backyard lights require electrical permits in Texas?
Most Texas municipalities require permits for permanent outdoor lighting installations involving new circuits. Low-voltage landscape lighting may be exempt. Your licensed electrician handles permit applications and scheduling inspections as part of comprehensive service.
How long does under cabinet lighting installation take?
Installing below cabinet lighting, including led tape light under cabinet or hardwired under cabinet led strip systems, generally takes three to six hours. Factors include kitchen size, power source location, and whether you choose plug-in or direct-wire lights for under counter areas.
Are LED fixtures worth the investment for Texas homes?
Absolutely. LED lighting reduces energy consumption by at least 75% compared to incandescent bulbs. Given Texas summer cooling costs, LED fixtures also generate less heat, easing air conditioning demands. Most LED installations pay for themselves within two years.
What safety considerations apply to bathroom lighting?
Bathroom lighting must meet specific code requirements for moisture exposure. Fixtures near showers require appropriate wet or damp ratings. Ground fault circuit interrupter protection is mandatory. Professional installation of bath lighting ensures compliance with these critical safety standards.
Can I install an under cabinet lamp myself?
Plug-in under cabinet lamp units can be DIY projects. However, hardwired installations involving new circuits require a licensed electrician. Improper wiring creates fire hazards and may void homeowner insurance coverage.
