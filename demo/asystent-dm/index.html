How it works

  • Official integration — runs on your own Instagram account via ManyChat and Meta’s messaging API. No shared passwords, no unofficial bots.
  • Answers from your materials — FAQ, price list and event details you approve. Russian and Polish detected automatically.
  • Honest about being AI — the first message says it’s your brand’s AI assistant.
  • Hands off to your team — complaints, special requests and anything unclear get a “needs human” status and a notification.
  • No cold DMs — it answers people who wrote to you or reacted to a story; follow-ups only within Instagram’s messaging window, one message, no chasing.
  • Group chats stay with your team.
  • Setup in 5–7 days, including tone of voice in both languages and a test week.
