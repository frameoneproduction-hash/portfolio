How this is built in GoHighLevel

The same setup in your own GHL account, done in this order. Nothing to install, no extra apps.

Community group with spaces

In Memberships → Communities create a private group and the four spaces (channels). Only the teacher can post in Announcements.

Sign-up page

In Sites → Funnels build one mobile-first page like this one, with your photo, your words and a short form (name, email, consent).

Automatic access

A workflow: trigger Form submitted → tag the contact → grant access to the community group → welcome email with the login link. No manual approvals.

Test sign-up

A test student signs up on a phone: the email arrives, the login works, the spaces are visible. Then the test contact is removed.

Short guide for the teacher

One page: how to post, pin a message, approve or remove a member, and the link to share with students.
