Why licensed matters in Texas

Texas law requires electrical work to be performed by TDLR-licensed electricians. Statewide, electricians averaged $35.07/hr in 2024 (Bureau of Labor Statistics) – skilled work with real accountability: license numbers you can verify, insurance, and permits pulled when the job needs one.

All contractors/providers are independent and TX Electrical Dispatch does not warrant or guarantee any work performed. It is the responsibility of the homeowner to verify that the hired contractor furnishes the necessary license and insurance required for the work being performed. All persons depicted in a photo or video are actors or models and not contractors listed on TX Electrical Dispatch.
