1. English → Polish: product page and Allegro title

The same approach I used on a recent store localization – 129 products translated from English and 95 existing Polish ones proofread (client data not shown): one glossary for the whole catalog, option names translated too, numbers and units in Polish format, facts kept exactly as in the source.

English · source

Northfold Trail Merino Hiking Socks – 2-Pack

Cushioned merino wool socks for long days on the trail. The wool keeps your feet warm in the cold and dry when you sweat, and it doesn't hold odours, so you can wear them more than once between washes. Reinforced heel and toe, flat toe seam, no slipping inside the boot.

  • 70% merino wool, 27% polyamide, 3% elastane
  • Medium cushioning under the foot
  • Sizes: S (EU 35–38), M (EU 39–42), L (EU 43–46)
  • Machine wash at 30°C, do not tumble dry

Size: S / M / L · Colour: Charcoal / Moss green

Free shipping on orders over $50. 30-day returns.

Polski · translation

Skarpety trekkingowe z wełny merino Northfold Trail – 2 pary

Amortyzowane skarpety z wełny merino na długie dni na szlaku. Wełna grzeje, gdy jest zimno, i odprowadza wilgoć, gdy się pocisz. Nie zatrzymuje nieprzyjemnych zapachów, więc skarpety możesz założyć więcej niż raz między praniami. Wzmocniona pięta i palce, płaski szew przy palcach, skarpety nie zsuwają się w bucie.

  • Skład: 70% wełna merino, 27% poliamid, 3% elastan
  • Średnia amortyzacja pod stopą
  • Rozmiary: S (35–38), M (39–42), L (43–46)
  • Pranie w pralce w 30°C, nie suszyć w suszarce bębnowej

Rozmiar: S / M / L · Kolor: grafitowy / zielony mech

Darmowa dostawa od [kwota w zł]*. 30 dni na zwrot.

* The free-shipping threshold in złoty is the shop owner's decision. I don't convert prices on my own.

Allegro listing title (max 75 characters)

Skarpety trekkingowe merino Northfold Trail 2 pary 39-42 grafitowe – 66 characters

Allegro takes colour and size from its own value lists. If the exact shade isn't on the list, I pick the closest value and keep the shade in the title.

What changed and why
  • “2-Pack” → “2 pary”. Polish shops count socks in pairs. “2-pak” sounds like a direct copy from English.
  • “EU 35–38” → “35–38”. EU sizes are the standard in Poland, so the label is not needed.
  • “you” → “Ty”. Most Polish online stores talk to customers informally. If your brand uses the formal “Pan/Pani”, I keep that across the whole catalog.
  • Option values translated. Customers filter by “Kolor” and “Rozmiar”, so the option names are translated too, not only the description.
  • Facts unchanged. 30-day returns stay 30 days, materials and percentages exactly as in the source. If the source contradicts itself, you get a list instead of a silent guess.

3. Russian → Polish and English

A short product text for a fictional handmade ceramics shop.

Русский · source

Кружка ручной работы, 350 мл. Каждая глазурь чуть отличается, поэтому двух одинаковых кружек не бывает. Можно мыть в посудомоечной машине, в микроволновку не ставить.

Polski

Kubek wykonany ręcznie, 350 ml. Każde szkliwo wychodzi trochę inaczej, dlatego nie ma dwóch takich samych kubków. Można myć w zmywarce, nie nadaje się do kuchenki mikrofalowej.

English

Handmade mug, 350 ml. Every glaze turns out a little different, so no two mugs are the same. Dishwasher safe, not microwave safe.

Northfold and the ceramics shop are fictional, made up for these samples. Made for Fiverr and Upwork clients.
