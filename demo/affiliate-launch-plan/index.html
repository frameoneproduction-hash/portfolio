Affiliate program launch

Refgrow + Paddle: from referral link to paid commission

A launch plan for two digital products sold through Paddle: Studio Course (one-time, $199) and Studio Club (subscription, $29/month). Fictional products. The same plan works for your two products once I have your prices and commission rules.

1. How a sale reaches the affiliate

  1. ClickAffiliate shares yoursite.com/?ref=ANNA10
  2. CookieRefgrow script stores the code for 30 days (cookie + localStorage backup)
  3. CheckoutThe code goes into Paddle as custom_data.referral_code, or via the payment-link helper
  4. WebhookPaddle sends transaction.completed to Refgrow, signed with your secret
  5. CommissionProduct rate applied, held for the refund window, then released for payout

Coupon codes are a second path: a buyer who never clicked a link can still be attributed through the affiliate's coupon.

3. Setup checklist

  • Tracking script in the <head> of every marketing and checkout page
  • Paddle API key with Notifications (read/write), Transactions, Subscriptions and Customers (read)
  • Webhook in Paddle to Refgrow's URL for transaction.completed, subscription.created, subscription.updated; signing secret pasted into Refgrow
  • Referral code passed in custom_data on every checkout, including payment links
  • Product rates on both products, hold period, payout methods and minimum
  • Affiliate portal: public signup or invite-only, logo and name, resources and links
  • Admin access stays on your accounts; keys stored in Paddle and Refgrow only
  • Sandbox first, then the same tests on live with a small real purchase

5. Gaps I would flag before launch

  • Emails. Refgrow's email docs list three automatic emails (new referral signup, conversion, payout) and say nothing about welcome or approval emails, so those may need a separate step.
  • Portal branding. The hosted portal shows your logo and name; full brand colours are available in the embedded widget.
  • Cross-domain checkout. If checkout runs on another domain, the code has to travel in the URL or checkout data, or the cookie is lost.
