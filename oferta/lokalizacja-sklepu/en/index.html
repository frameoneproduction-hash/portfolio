What localisation includes

Product pages

Names, descriptions, attributes and variants. Collection and brand names stay; everything else reads naturally in the target language.

Shop texts

Standard texts: menu, categories, filters, cart, messages, footer and order confirmation emails. No blog or information pages.

SEO for each country

Google titles and descriptions within character limits, URLs without special characters, names checked against local shops and Google suggestions.

Market notes

Sizes (a German duvet is 135×200, a Czech one 140×200), currency, number and temperature formats.

Ready to upload

The texts come back in the same layout you exported them in (CSV or XLSX), so you import them in one file.

Consistent glossary

A term list for your shop, so new products sound the same as the first hundred.

Translation vs localisation

Word-for-word translation

“Bettwäsche aus Leinen gewaschen mit Steinen 160×200”

Correct words, but nobody searches or writes like that.

Localisation

“Leinenbettwäsche grau, stonewashed”

The name German shops use, plus a note that their standard size is 155×220, not 160×200.
